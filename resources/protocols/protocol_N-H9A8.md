## Procedure
Use TargetAmp 2-round aminoallyl amplification kit from Epicentre biotechnologies.

Add an additional 3/4 ul of SBI semi-random primer at step one, before speed-vacing to
 3 ul.

Also, add 1 ul of this primer for part F, step one, in addition to the Epicentre primer.
