## Abstract
This protocol describes methods used used by the Editorial Office in the University of Edinburgh for manually checking microarray data in GUDMAP1, GUDMAP2 and nGUDMAP.

## Procedure
## Manual Checking Protocols
### **Sheet 1 / Sample Data Sheet incorporates sample & platform data**

**Unique ID**  
A Unique Identifier for each submission is given by the submitter.

**Person Details**  
Checks ensure that the Submitter, Authors, submitting lab and PI lab details are correct.

**Specimen Details**  
Tissue cell type, ontology name, genus / species, strain, mutation, pool size, sex, developmental stage and Theiler Stage are checked.  The ontology name is given as an EMAP ID and this needs to correlate with the Theiler Stage.  The developmental stage given by the submitter should match the appropriate Theiler Stage for GUDMAP.

**Sample Image**  
Sample image sent must illustrate the source of the sample eg. laser capture or the quality of the FACS process. 

**RNA Quality Details**  
Details checked.

**Platform Details**  
Checks with the Affymetrix website ensure the accuracy of information given.

**Experimental Goals, Experimental Designs and Dissection Method**  
Checks ensure the accuracy of the information given.

**Associated Files**  
Checks carried out of the details of associated files that support the array submission i.e. CEL, CHP, RPT, EXP and TXT files. This ensures that the correct associated files are uploaded to the entry.

**Non_Wildtype Details**  
Checks carried out using other databases. eg. MMRRC, PUBMED

**Transgenic Reporter Alleles**  
Checks carried out using other databases. eg. MMRRC, PUBMED

### Sheet 2 / Series Data Sheet **contains the series data**

Series data links together groups of samples and describe study as a whole.

**Standard Fields Table**  
Table contains information relating to the design of the experiment and requires both a tile and summary.  Checks ensure the accuracy of information given.

**Sample ID Table**  
Table contains the Unique ID from the Sample Data Sheet along with a description of that sample.  Checks ensure the accuracy of information given.

**Variable Table**  
Table follows the GEO archive metadata guidelines.  Checks ensure the accuracy of information given.

**Replicates Table**  
Table follows the GEO archive metadata guidelines.  Checked ensure the accuracy of information given.
